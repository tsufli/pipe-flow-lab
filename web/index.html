What is being simulated?

A 3D incompressible Navier–Stokes velocity field in a circular pipe with no-slip walls and axial periodicity. Pipe radius is 1, the laminar centerline speed is 1, and the bulk speed is 1/2. The parameter Re uses bulk speed and pipe diameter (equivalently centerline speed and radius).

Baseline laminar profile: uz(r) = 1 − r². An optional localized finite-amplitude roll perturbs it. Flow rate is held fixed, allowing the pressure gradient to respond to turbulent drag.

Laminar dissipation Dlam = 2/Re and Darcy friction fD,lam = 64/Re. dnsjax stores the excess axial driving −dP/dz′, so fD = 64/Re + 16(−dP/dz′).

Turbulence intensity shown for snapshots is the cross-sectional RMS of all three velocity fluctuations about their azimuthal–axial mean profiles, divided by Ubulk. This is not the same as the disturbance energy E′, which includes changes to the mean profile.

A four-diameter periodic box is a minimal proof-of-concept and generally cannot reproduce long localized turbulent puffs or measure an intrinsic transition Reynolds number. For research-quality claims, test larger boxes, spatial and temporal resolution, and ensembles.

Solver: dnsjax (Apache-2.0). Visualization quantities are reconstructed from saved velocity fields.
