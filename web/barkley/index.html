Numerical method and interpretation

Fourth-order Runge–Kutta (RK4) with a user-controlled step size. The trajectory is computed independently on each parameter update. Results are intended for exploring smooth local dynamics; convergence can be checked by halving Δt. The vector arrows are normalized to emphasize direction, not speed. The nonnegative half-plane q ≥ 0 is invariant; calculations are not artificially clipped. The parameter defaults are from Barkley (2016), equations (8.14)–(8.16), with U₀=2 and Ū=1 in the chosen frame. For the local dynamics, advection, diffusion and stochastic forcing are omitted. Fixed points are intersections of the nullclines, with stability determined by the two-dimensional Jacobian.
