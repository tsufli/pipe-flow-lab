What should agree?

The analytical construction matches leading-order upstream and downstream front velocities, integrates the slow turbulent interior, and excludes front widths of order unity. Its length is not defined by the q = 0.5 crossings. The deterministic finite-ε threshold width is a more directly comparable reference, though stochastic noise can shift the mean and inferred equilibrium length.

How the measurements are defined

dq = A(ℓ)dt + B(ℓ)dW;   D = BBᵀ/2
Ai(ℓ) ≈ E[Δxi | ℓ] / Δt,   Dij(ℓ) ≈ Cov(Δxi, Δxj | ℓ) / (2Δt)
Aℓ = Ad − Au,   Dℓ = Ddd + Duu − 2Dud.

Quality control

Research limitations. A finite-time conditional drift is not proof of a Markovian diffusion or OU process. The Barkley u field is a hidden slow variable. The instantaneous measured front position is affected by q threshold and spatial smoothing; data at large noise can fragment. Binned intervals are temporally correlated, so uncertainty is bootstrapped across whole realizations (not individual frames). The data are at one space-time resolution and 100 realizations only; no ε→0, Δx→0, Δt→0, lag→0, or noise-amplitude convergence is claimed. Censoring on surviving puffs changes the inferred dynamics near collapse.

Reproduce with python scripts/barkley_front_tracking.py --runs 100 --T 120 --sigma 0.5 --seed 20261123. Public source and data: GitHub repository folder. Analytical baseline: Frishman & Grafke (2022), section III.C. The physical model is a 1D reduced stochastic Barkley model, not Navier–Stokes DNS.
