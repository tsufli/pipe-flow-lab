Scientific and numerical details

Spatial stochastic Barkley model (8.14), Itô term σqη, white in space and time. Physical parameters: ζ=0.8, D=0.5, δ=0.1, ε₁=0.1, ε₂=0.2, U₀=2, Ū=1. The numerical solver uses explicit Euler–Maruyama, periodic boundary conditions, an upwind discretization for q-advection and a conservative Rusanov flux for u-advection. Each AMS run starts from a dynamically evolved, stochastically aged puff.

AMS repeatedly kills the trajectory with the least progress toward laminar flow and replaces it by branching from the first crossing of its score, with fresh independent noise. The reaction coordinate is the maximum fractional decrease of integrated positive q. Decay is declared when ∫max(q,0)dx ≤ 0.15. The finite-horizon event is decay by time H, not an arbitrary puff-length threshold.

Validation required before quantitative use: repeat AMS with independent seeds and confidence intervals; verify that survival is exponential and that inferred τ is independent of H; compare against ordinary simulation at low r; test Δx/Δt, puff preparation and decay thresholds; control splitting as a competing event. The published Barkley fit is shown for comparison only, not used to calibrate any AMS point.
