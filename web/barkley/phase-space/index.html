Full spatial ODE (not the local time-evolution ODE)

dq/dz = p   ;   D dp/dz = (u − ζ − c)p − f(q,u)   ;   (u − c)du/dz = εG(q,u)
f(q,u) = q[r + u − U₀ − (r + δ)(q − 1)²]   ;   G(q,u) = (U₀ − u) + κ(Ū − u)q

Default constants: U₀ = 2, Ū = 1, ζ = 0.8, δ = 0.1, D = 0.5, κ = 2. No noise. ε is the slow-evolution parameter. Here r is a model Reynolds parameter, not the physical Reynolds number.

Interpretation and numerical caveats

The selected p-slice shows f=0 and G=0 (local reaction nullclines), plus the true spatial p′=0 cross-section whenever p≠0. Spatial q′=0 means p=0. The laminar fixed point is L=(q,p,u)=(0,0,U₀). Any turbulent fixed points T=(qₛₛ,0,uₛₛ) are intersections of f=0 and G=0. A genuine puff is a homoclinic orbit approaching L as z→±∞ at a selected c. Such a connection is sensitive to c and cannot be inferred from a visually closed projected curve. The auto-shoot routine starts along the unstable eigendirection of L and searches c using numerical trajectories; it reports the outcome rather than pretending an imperfect return is a true homoclinic. Slug front connections L→T and T→L, when they exist, generally occur at different front speeds and are not necessarily simultaneous orbits of a single fixed-c spatial system. Numerical trajectories stop if they leave the plotted physical region or approach the singular surface u=c. Step-size sensitivity can be tested by comparing runs. Fixed points and both nullclines update with r. Source: Barkley (2016), §§8–9, especially equations (9.4) and (9.6).
