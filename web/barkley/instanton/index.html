What has actually been minimized

Searching for a low-action escape route

The noise directly forces q; u evolves by its deterministic PDE. We optimize the position, spatial width and duration of a localized negative-noise pulse, then tune its amplitude to the puff/laminar basin boundary. The resulting trajectories approach the computed decay edge.

Change the parameter, switch between a baseline and lower-action pulse, and scrub along the path to understand the transition. At the edge, inspect the unstable departure in either direction.

Scientific status: restricted-family candidate, not a verified global instanton.

The reported cost is minimized only over the tested Gaussian pulse family; it is not a solution of the unrestricted minimum-action problem. Reverse deterministic paths and puff–edge linear interpolation are provided as geometric references only because they generally violate the unforced u equation.

How to interpret what you're seeing

The main plot shows a real numerical trajectory generated by adding the displayed noise control to the Barkley q equation. The u field follows the deterministic equation at every integration step. The edge shown as a reference is spatially aligned for shape comparison only.

Action, with multiplicative noise
qₜ = Fq[q,u] + q a(x,t)
uₜ = Fu[q,u]
I[a] = ½ ∫₀ᵀ dt ∫ dx a(x,t)²
Probability ∝ exp(−I/σ²)

The displayed I excludes the overall factor 1/σ². All action values are evaluated on the N=320 numerical grid using Δx=0.5, in the puff's co-moving coordinate. The noise is spatially and temporally white under the grid discretization.

What is meant by the reversed deterministic path?

The independently computed edge has a positive unstable eigenvalue. A perturbation along its growing eigenvector runs from the edge toward the puff without noise. Reversing that history is a useful geometric candidate, but it does not in general solve uₜ=Fu and therefore does not have a finite stochastic action in this degenerate-noise model. The overlay matches turbulent mass and aligns spatial peaks for shape comparison only.

How was the action optimized?

The optimization scans Gaussian pulse centers, widths and durations, followed by local Nelder–Mead refinement. For each trial shape, its amplitude is found by bisection to the puff/laminar boundary. All retained trajectories satisfy the controlled q equation and deterministic u equation. This is a restricted-family minimum, not gMAM or an unrestricted Freidlin–Wentzell minimizer.

Numerical record

Deterministic traveling puff and edge states were recomputed at r=0.5 and r=0.6 with the same second-order backward advection scheme and centered q diffusion, then integrated with controlled RK4 trajectories. The control-search basins were tested at t=240; near the boundary, the solution shadows the decay edge before eventually departing.

Visible trajectory profiles are downsampled from 320 to 160 cells for faster browsing. Action and distance diagnostics are evaluated at full grid resolution before downsampling. The end of the plotted path is not a certified infinite-time arrival at the edge.
